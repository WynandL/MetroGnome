**What is completely absent from the log, never once mentioned in any session:** self-serve

That is the pattern worth acting on, and it points at two untouched levers.

### Trend check - what is actually live right now

Honest answer: **no new music trend worth chasing surfaced this session.** The TikTok trend
searches returned dance and lip-sync formats ("What's Going On", "The Chair", Tyler the Creator
audio) with no rhythm-skill hook. The "Say the Word on Beat" trend was already caught on 08-03
and the post is live. Odd meters and polyrhythms show steady evergreen interest (Tool, Dream
Theater, Brubeck framing recurring across sources) but no 2026 surge - and that cluster is
already covered by an explainer plus a tool, with a standing "do not write a third piece" rule.

The genuinely useful trend finding is not a music trend. It is a **category-behaviour** one:

**Every established free-metronome site in this space distributes itself as an embeddable
widget.** Confirmed live this session: 4four.io publishes a documented `<iframe>` embed snippet,
guitarapp.com generates embed code for both a metronome and a tuner and requires an attribution
link beneath each one, tonal.fm offers a parameterised embed with BPM and beats-per-bar options,
and bestmetronome.com has a dedicated `/simpleembed` page. Metro Gnome has three working browser
tools with the best conversion rate on the site and **no embed path at all.**

That is the gap. It also happens to be the only backlink mechanism that does not require an
editor's permission: every teacher, band director or lesson site that embeds a widget publishes
the attribution link themselves.

Checked and confirmed: **`landing/vercel.json` sets no `X-Frame-Options` or CSP headers**, so
the pages are already iframe-embeddable today. No hosting change is required to make this work.

## RECOMMENDATION - ship an embeddable widget version of the three tools

**Why this and not something else:** it attacks the one bottleneck the log has diagnosed for
three months (no backlinks -> position stuck), through the one route that has actually worked
this month (a change the dev controls entirely), applied to the one format the data says
converts (tools at 6.1% vs blog at 0.50%). It needs no editor, no new content topic, and no
new engine code - the three tools already exist and already work.

### Files to create

**1. `landing/embed/odd-meter.html`, `landing/embed/tap-tempo.html`, `landing/embed/rhythm-test.html`**

Each is a stripped copy of its `landing/tools/` source. Concretely, from
`landing/tools/odd-meter-practice-tool.html` (322 lines):

- **Keep:** the `<head>` block, `<section class="tool-panel">` (line 137 to its close), and the
  entire inline `<script>` (line 322 to end). These are self-contained and need no changes.
- **Delete:** `<nav class="site-nav">` (lines 100-115), `<nav class="breadcrumb">` (117-124),
  `<header class="article-header">` (125-135), all prose sections and related-cards below the
  tool panel, and `<footer>` (309-320).
- **Change in `<head>`:** replace `<link rel="canonical" href=".../tools/odd-meter-practice-tool.html">`
  with `<link rel="canonical" href="https://metrognome.co.za/tools/odd-meter-practice-tool.html">`
  pointing at the full tool (so the embed never competes with its own parent page in the index),
  and add `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, follow">`. `noindex` keeps thin duplicate widget
  pages out of the index; `follow` keeps the link equity flowing.
- **Add** at the bottom of the panel, in place of the deleted footer:

```html
<p class="embed-credit">
  <a href="https://metrognome.co.za/tools/odd-meter-practice-tool.html?ref=embed"
     target="_blank" rel="noopener">Odd meter metronome</a>
  by <a href="https://metrognome.co.za/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Metro Gnome</a>
</p>
```

Anchor text differs per widget: "Odd meter metronome", "Tap tempo BPM finder", "Rhythm timing
test" - three distinct keyword-relevant anchors rather than three identical ones. Both links
dofollow. The `?ref=embed` parameter makes embed-sourced referral traffic measurable in GSC and
GA4 without any tracking code.

**2. `landing/embed/style.css`**

A compact stylesheet, not `blog/style.css`. The embed must render inside a ~400x600px iframe on
somebody else's page, so it needs a transparent-friendly background, no max-width container, and
tighter padding. Roughly 60-80 lines, lifting only the `.tool-panel` rules already in
`blog/style.css` plus the `.embed-credit` rule (small, muted, centred).

**3. `landing/embed/index.html` - the page that does the SEO work**

This is the one indexable page in the directory, and the actual asset. Title:
`Free Metronome & Rhythm Tool Embeds for Your Site - Metro Gnome`. Contents:

- One paragraph: these are free to embed on lesson pages, band-programme sites and studio sites;
  no sign-up, no key, attribution link included.
- Three sections, one per tool, each with a live preview and a **click-to-copy code box**:

```html
<iframe src="https://metrognome.co.za/embed/odd-meter.html"
        width="100%" height="620" style="border:0;max-width:480px"
        title="Odd meter metronome by Metro Gnome" loading="lazy"></iframe>
```

- A short "terms" line: free for any site, keep the attribution link, do not modify the code.
  (This is what guitarapp.com does, and it is what makes the link durable.)
- Targets an uncovered query cluster: `embed metronome on website`, `metronome widget for
  website`, `free metronome iframe`, `metronome for teachers website`. Checked against all 16
  existing posts and all 3 tool pages - zero overlap, entirely net-new surface area.

**8. `landing/vercel.json`** - optional but recommended, add explicitly so a future default or
platform change cannot silently break every live embed:

```json
"headers": [
  {
    "source": "/embed/(.*)",
    "headers": [
      { "key": "Content-Security-Policy", "value": "frame-ancestors *" },
      { "key": "X-Frame-Options", "value": "" }
    ]
  }
]
```

### Effort and honest expectations

Roughly a half-day. The three widget files are extractions from working code, not new
functionality; `embed/index.html` is the only page needing real authoring.

The honest part: **embed adoption is slow and may never come at all.** This is not a fix that
lands next week. What makes it worth doing anyway is the asymmetry - it costs one afternoon, it
requires nobody's approval, it never needs maintenance once shipped, and each single embed that
does happen is a permanent contextual dofollow link from a music-education site, which is
precisely the link type all seven dead outreach threads were chasing. One adoption beats the
entire outreach programme's three-month return of zero.

Built and pushed as `def0a1e` on master, Vercel auto-deploying. Live at

**What went out:**

- `landing/embed/index.html` - the indexable asset. Live previews, copy-to-clipboard snippets, a

- `vercel.json` declares `frame-ancestors *` on `/embed/`. Nothing set `X-Frame-Options` before, so
  the pages already embedded, but a live embed elsewhere would break silently and permanently if
  that default ever changed.

**Two things that were bugs before they were decisions**, both worth remembering:

1. **The credit line had to move inside `.tool-panel`.** It was first placed below the panel, on
   the transparent widget body. On a white lesson page, near-white credit text on white is an
   invisible credit, which defeats the entire purpose of the exercise. It now sits on the panel's
   own dark gradient with a hairline divider, legible on any host background.
2. **The iframe heights are measured, not guessed.** The first guesses were up to 140px short and
   produced scrollbars in every preview. They were measured in-browser at 480px and 300px, then
   had to be re-measured after the credit moved inside the panel and added height.

**Verified in a real browser before shipping**, not assumed: widgets render and play; switching to
5/4 regroups to 2+3 with the label and pulse visualisation updating mid-playback, so the ported
`Meter.kt` logic survived extraction; the tap-tempo tempo markings still resolve (51 BPM to Largo,
60 to Larghetto) from the reference table the generator carries across hidden, which was the one
real risk in the extraction; the credit reads correctly on a white host page; copy buttons work;
no console errors; and all three heights now fit with no scrollbars.

**Honest expectation, unchanged from this morning: adoption is slow and may never come.** Do not
judge this by the next pull, and do not rewrite it if it has no embeds after a month. It cost an
afternoon, needs no maintenance beyond re-running the generator, and one adoption beats the
outreach programme's three-month return of zero.
