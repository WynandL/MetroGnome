>
> Following up on my note from June 2 about Metro Gnome for your "Best Metronome Apps for Android" article.
>
> One update worth mentioning: the app just shipped a feature that might make it more distinctive for the roundup. It now uses the phone's microphone to track how closely you land on the beat while you practice. It separates your claps or playing from the click by frequency (not volume), corrects for each device's audio latency, and gives you a Groove Score at the end of the session. It runs entirely on-device with no account. No other free Android metronome does this to my knowledge.
