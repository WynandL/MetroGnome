## Market Scan

### Competitor News

**Metronome Beats (Stonekick)** — Updated May 19 2026 (2 days ago). New: cross-device preset/setlist/song sync and MIDI device support. Free tier retains full features. 4.8 stars / 172K reviews. Raised the bar on cross-device continuity — MetroGnome's SharedPreferences-only persistence could become friction as installs grow.

**Pro Metronome (EUMLab)** — No update this month. Active unresolved complaints: timing inconsistency ("drags and rushes"), poor beat tones, IAP error loops, subscription bait-and-switch, confusing UI with no in-app instructions. A significant number of users are actively looking for alternatives.

**Soundbrenner Metronome** — Updated April 10 2026. Added bar/duration auto-pause and playback counter. Hardware-wearable ecosystem focus; not a direct threat for a lean app.

### Reddit / Forum Pain Points

- Swing mode glitchy or broken on major apps
- App updates wiping saved song libraries with no backup
- Ads interrupting practice flow — musicians want minimal, unobtrusive free tiers
- Pro Metronome timing inconsistency cited as dealbreaker for drummers
- Pano Tuner praised (Feb 2026) specifically for "opens straight to tuner, no sign-ins or fluff" — frictionless launch is valued
- Subscription bait-and-switch actively generating user resentment

### Opportunity Gaps Identified

1. **Timing reliability** — Pro Metronome's inconsistency is a live complaint. MetroGnome's raw AudioTrack engine is a genuine technical differentiator — undersold in current listing copy.
2. **Integrated metronome + tuner, no account required** — competitors silo the two tools; MetroGnome uniquely combines them with zero friction.
3. **No subscription, ever** — one-time IAP model is a trust signal given current Pro Metronome backlash.
4. **Rhythm game** — no top competitor has one; directly addresses "practice is boring."
5. **Cross-device sync gap** — Stonekick just shipped this; worth watching as a future retention feature.

## Recommendations

### a) ASO Suggestion
**Add "No subscription. Ever." and "Save custom BPM presets" to the Play Store long description.**

The Pro Metronome subscription backlash is active right now. MetroGnome already has a one-time IAP model — stating this explicitly is a conversion signal for users burned by competitors. Additionally, "BPM presets" is a concrete feature that exists in code but appears nowhere in the current listing; adding it targets a real search intent ("metronome with presets") and gives users a concrete reason to choose MetroGnome over a simpler tool.

Proposed addition to the long description (insert near the features list):
> "Save your favourite BPM presets — recall any tempo instantly. No subscription, ever: one-time purchase to remove ads, that's it."

### b) Content Idea
**Blog post: "Why your metronome app might be lying to you"**

Angle: explains how most apps (implicitly referencing the pain point from Reddit/reviews) use `Handler.postDelayed` or `Timer` on Android, which drifts under CPU load — and how MetroGnome uses a raw `AudioTrack` write loop for sample-accurate timing. Include a plain-language explainer (no jargon), a diagram of the timing drift problem, and a 30-second practice drill to hear the difference. Post on metrognome.co.za/blog and share to r/WeAreTheMusicMakers and r/drums as a resource comment (not a promo). This directly captures the audience actively complaining about Pro Metronome's timing.

### c) Code Improvement — Streak Visibility for Retention
**Surface the practice streak on the Metronome home screen.**

`PracticeSessionManager` already tracks consecutive-day streaks but this data is invisible to the user outside the practice timer UI. Adding a small "🔥 3-day streak" badge or chip below the BPM display costs ~20 lines of Compose and has outsized retention impact: streak visibility is one of the highest-ROI retention mechanics (Duolingo's entire model). Day 1→Day 7 retention directly influences Play Store ranking algorithm. A user who sees their streak is a user who returns tomorrow.

Implementation sketch:
- Read `currentStreak: Int` from `PracticeSessionManager` in `MetronomeViewModel`
- Expose via `StateFlow<Int>`
- In `MetronomeScreen`, show a `Text("🔥 $streak-day streak")` chip (hidden when streak == 0)
