## Recommendation - ACTIONED TODAY: removed a fabricated AggregateRating from the homepage schema, added disambiguating sameAs

This is outside the standard daily-research checklist - found while looking at the homepage's structured data for an unrelated reason (checking whether an Organization/sameAs entity link could help the already-diagnosed "metrognome" brand-collision CTR ceiling, see below) and it's material enough to fix same-day rather than queue.

### What was wrong

`landing/index.html`'s `SoftwareApplication` JSON-LD has carried a hardcoded `aggregateRating` (`ratingValue: 4.5`, `ratingCount: 1000`) since **2026-05-20** (commit `59ea01c1`) - roughly three months, through every SEO session since, none of which caught it. `seo_context.md`'s own App section has said "Rating: not yet established at scale" the entire time this was live. The `ratingCount: 1000` value is almost certainly a copy-paste of the "1,000+" Play Store install-count bucket, not an actual review count - the two numbers aren't the same thing and there's no evidence the app has 1,000 ratings.

This is not a cosmetic issue. Google's structured data guidelines require review/rating markup to reflect genuine reviews of the actual entity ("markup must reflect actual reviews of the app"), and fabricated or placeholder rating markup is a spam-policy violation (misleading/fake engagement markup). The realistic risk isn't a search penalty on this specific page - it's a **manual action that strips rich-result eligibility sitewide** if it's ever caught by a review. Three months live with zero downside noticed is not evidence it's safe, just evidence it hasn't been checked yet.

### What changed

- **Removed** the fabricated `aggregateRating` block entirely from `landing/index.html`. Sitewide search confirmed no other page carries the same block.
- **Added** `"sameAs": ["https://metrognome.co.za/"]` to the same schema, alongside the existing `url` field (which already points to the Play Store listing). This links the two canonical "Metro Gnome" surfaces (Play Store entity, website entity) under one schema, a legitimate, low-cost technical nudge toward Google's entity disambiguation - directly relevant to the already-diagnosed "metrognome" collision with the unrelated 249K-subscriber YouTube channel (logged 08-10 as a CTR ceiling "not fixable via title/meta/content"). `sameAs` is a different lever than any of those three, so it doesn't contradict that finding - it's genuinely untried. Framed conservatively: this is a plausible, unproven nudge on a problem already diagnosed as structurally hard, not a claimed fix. One-directional `sameAs` link (no reciprocal profiles exist to add) is weaker signal than a full cluster, but it's free and correct, so there's no reason not to have it.
- Verified both JSON-LD blocks on the homepage still parse cleanly after the edit.

### If real rating data becomes available later

If/when the Play Store rating is checked and it's good enough to be worth surfacing (this is exactly the kind of "specific gap real data would resolve" the daily process asks about), re-add `aggregateRating` with the **real** `ratingValue` and `ratingCount` pulled from the Play Store listing at that time - never a placeholder again.
