## Root cause of zero impressions

No external backlinks. Google's crawl budget is weighted by domain authority.
A new domain with no external sites linking to it gets deprioritised, regardless
of content quality. The pages are discovered but not worth crawling yet in Google's
view.

## The one thing that unblocks this

External links. Every external site that links to metrognome.co.za — Reddit posts,
forum comments, a mention in a music Facebook group, any roundup inclusion — signals
legitimacy and increases crawl priority. This is why the Reddit posts and outreach
matter beyond just traffic: they are link signals.
