## Recommendation - "Groove Score Challenge": a shareable score card built entirely from features that already exist

**The problem with the obvious answer.** More outreach emails and more blog posts are the right long-term SEO play, but they are inherently slow (weeks-to-months) and linear (each email reaches one editor). They cannot "go viral quickly," which is what was asked. Also ruled out: paid UA (explicitly excluded), and posting more Reddit self-promo (thin ice - one subreddit already permanently bans the app for this, per seo_context.md).

**The angle.** TikTok and Reddit both currently have a live, proven content template for rhythm content: *"only 1% of people can stay on beat"* - a fixed, comparable timing task + a score + a dare to beat it. Metro Gnome already has the entire scoring engine for this built and shipped: Groove Check (`groove/GrooveScorer.kt`, `groove/SessionAnalyzer.kt`) produces a 0-100 `grooveScore` from real mic timing analysis, already surfaced in the post-session overlay (`ui/overlays/PracticeCompleteOverlay.kt:133-140` via `PerformanceBonusReward`). What's missing is the one thing that makes a score go anywhere: **a way to take it out of the app and show it to someone else.**

**What to build (v1, small):**

1. **A fixed "Challenge" task**, not open-ended practice. Comparability is what makes Wordle-style scores shareable - everyone must be scored on the same thing. Concrete spec: 90 BPM, 4/4, 20 seconds (roughly matches the existing "20-second demo" already called out as length-independent in `groove/GrooveScorer.kt:19`), Classic click, claps only. This can be a new lightweight mode, or literally a preset Practice session with a fixed duration and mic mode forced on - reuses the entire existing engine, no new detection code.

2. **A share button on the result**, added next to the existing "Sweet!" dismiss button in `ui/overlays/PracticeCompleteOverlay.kt:144-161` (and the equivalent in `ui/overlays/SpeedTrainerResultOverlay.kt`), visible only when `result.grooveScore > 0`. Standard `Intent.ACTION_SEND` with `EXTRA_STREAM` - no backend, no account, consistent with the app's existing "on-device, no account" positioning. (Confirmed: no share-intent code exists anywhere in the app today - this is new, but small - a `Bitmap` render of the score card plus a chooser intent, roughly a half-day of work.)

3. **The card itself** reuses an asset that already exists for exactly this purpose: `ui/components/Seal.kt`, the app's "earned and verified" rosette-with-check mark, already used for Groove Check passes elsewhere. Stamp the score card with a Seal so a screenshot reads as a verified result, not a screenshotted claim. Card content: big score number, "Groove Score", the Metro gnome mascot, and the challenge parameters (90 BPM / 4/4 / 20s) so it's self-explanatory out of context.

4. **Share text (write-once, ship with the button):**
   > I scored [X]/100 on Metro Gnome's Groove Score Challenge. Think you can hold a beat better than me? [Play Store link]

5. **Seed it personally first** (cost: zero, this week): record 15-20 seconds of the challenge + share card on a phone, post to r/drums (re-entry is eligible - 42+ days clear per the table above) and TikTok with the "only 1% can stay on beat" framing already proven to work in that space. One authentic post from the dev, not a campaign.

**Why this beats another roundup email for "quickly":** a roundup email has a ceiling of one editor's reply. A share card has no ceiling - every session played by every existing user becomes a possible outbound link, for free, forever, and it's aimed at a content format that is already trending rather than one Metro Gnome has to invent. It also sidesteps the brand-name search collision found today (Research Summary) - a shared link goes straight to the Play Store listing, it doesn't depend on winning a "metrognome" search against two older namesake apps.

**Sequencing:** this doesn't block the overdue roundup outreach - send those this week regardless, they cost nothing. This is the next build slot.
