### Standout finding — Soundcorset (Tuner & Metronome) tuner reliability

Soundcorset is the **closest format competitor** (combined tuner + metronome, free with ads) and the most useful signal this week. Its 2026 reviews show a consistent, specific complaint pattern about the **tuner**, not the metronome:

- Tuner **incorrectly picks up harmonics** and reports the wrong note.
- **Fails to track pitch changes in real time** / becomes unresponsive.
- "Takes forever for it to listen when you play" — delayed reaction described as **"broken and useless."**
- Share button demands a "Soundcorset ID" login, then traps the user on a broken login screen.

This is a direct, public validation of Metro Gnome's two biggest tuner differentiators:
1. **AmbientDetector** (room profiling + 1500ms fast re-acquire) → answers the "takes forever to listen" complaint.
2. The **harmonic-ratio-contamination fix** in calibration (session 2026-05-17) → answers the "picks up harmonics / wrong note" complaint.

The leading combo app's single weakest feature is exactly the feature Metro Gnome does best. That is a content + ASO opening.

## Codebase Findings

### Version drift — listing/context is two releases behind

`seo_context.md` says **"Last updated: May 18 2026"** (v4.2). `app/build.gradle.kts` is now **versionCode 29 / versionName 4.4**. Two cosmetic releases shipped since the last report and are not reflected in the context doc or (presumably) the listing:

- **v4.3** (`bae6e9b`) — Hat Plume cosmetic + full item visual overhaul.
- **v4.4** (`5731b77`) — Street Lantern redesign, Glowing Mushroom cluster, Moon & Stars upgrade, custom `StreakIcon`, centralized unlock logic, **and ad-frequency capping**.

### New & promotable: ad-frequency capping (v4.4)

`ads/InterstitialAdManager.kt:42-46` now caps interstitials to **every 3rd game AND at most once per 5 minutes**. Given that ad-*placement* is the explicit line between tolerated and hated in competitor reviews (Soundcorset tolerated, Metronome Beats criticized), this is both a genuine retention improvement and a defensible marketing claim ("respectful, non-intrusive ads"). Currently promoted nowhere.

### Code concern: in-app review prompt fires at the worst possible moment

`review/AppReviewManager.kt` is solid (3-distinct-day gate, once-only, all Play errors swallowed). **But** `MainActivity.kt:77-80` calls `maybeRequestReview()` inside `ON_RESUME` — so the native Play rating sheet pops the instant the app is foregrounded on the 3rd day, **before the user has played a single beat that session**. Google's own guidance is to request a review after the user has experienced enough value, at a natural pause. Firing on cold resume maximises annoyance and depresses the rating it's trying to build. The May 20 report recommended *adding* this prompt; it now exists, but the trigger point is the weak link. (See Recommendation c.)

### Performance / stability

No red flags. `InterstitialAdManager` preloads once and reloads on dismiss/fail; `AppReviewManager` swallows all Play exceptions; audio remains on `Dispatchers.Default`. No new ANR/crash-prone patterns introduced by the v4.3/v4.4 cosmetic work (changes are Canvas-draw and SharedPreferences only).

## Recommendations

### a) ASO — reposition the tuner around *speed + lock-on*, not just "ambient noise detection"

Current listing describes the tuner passively ("ambient noise detection"). The Soundcorset complaint set shows the pain users actually feel is **slowness and wrong-note harmonics** — and they search/complain in those words. Update the tuner feature line to make the active claim:

> "Smart chromatic tuner that locks on fast and ignores background noise — tune in a loud rehearsal room without the lag or wrong-note guessing other tuners get stuck on."

Why this is genuinely better than current copy: it (1) targets the uncontested `chromatic tuner noisy room` keyword (480/mo, diff 14) *and* `smart tuner app android`, (2) converts the leading combo competitor's #1 weakness into a differentiated claim, and (3) costs nothing — it's a one-line swap, no name/short-description change needed.

### b) Content — blog post: "Why does my tuner lag or show the wrong note in a noisy room?"

Write a short explainer post on metrognome.co.za answering the exact question frustrated Soundcorset/GuitarTuna users type into search: harmonic confusion (why a low E reads as a higher note) and why most tuners stall in a noisy room. Explain the fix in plain terms — room profiling first, then fast re-acquire — and close with Metro Gnome as the app built that way. This (1) ranks for `chromatic tuner noisy room`, (2) is genuinely helpful (not an ad), and (3) gives a non-spammy link to drop when "my tuner is slow/wrong" threads appear on r/guitar, r/drums, r/violinist. Fresh angle — not covered by the existing three blog posts.

### c) Code — move the review prompt from `ON_RESUME` to a moment of delight

Highest-leverage change available right now. The app has 1,000+ installs and no established rating, so *when* the prompt fires materially affects the rating it earns.

1. **Remove** the `maybeRequestReview()` call from the `ON_RESUME` block in `MainActivity.kt:80` (keep `recordAppOpen()` there — day-counting should still happen on resume).
2. **Re-trigger it at a positive beat**, e.g. inside the practice-session completion overlay or the rhythm-game RESULT state after a good score — the existing eligibility gate (`>= 3 distinct days`, once-only) already prevents over-asking, so it stays a no-op until the user qualifies *and* hits the delight moment.
3. Net effect: same audience, far better timing → higher likelihood of a 4–5★ rating instead of an annoyed dismissal.

~10-line change, no new dependency, no new permission.
