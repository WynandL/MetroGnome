Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Metro Gnome really a free metronome app for Android?
Yes - completely free. The metronome, chromatic tuner, and rhythm game are all free with no time limits. There are optional purchases to remove ads or unlock cosmetic items, but the core tools never cost anything. Metro Gnome works on Android 7.0 and above, fully offline.
How does the BPM counter and tap tempo work?
Just tap the TAP button in rhythm - Metro Gnome calculates your BPM from the interval between taps (requires at least 2 taps within 2.5 seconds). You can also save any BPM as a named preset and switch between songs instantly. The metronome uses raw AudioTrack on the Android audio stack for sample-accurate timing that doesn't drift over long sessions.
Does the tuner work in a noisy practice room?
That's exactly what it was designed for. When you open the tuner, it spends a few seconds profiling your room's background noise. After that it distinguishes your instrument from the noise floor, rejects voices and broadband sounds, and only locks on to a stable musical tone. You'll see on-screen guidance (QUIET → ACQUIRING → LOCKED) so you always know what it's hearing. It works fine in rehearsal rooms, kitchens, or wherever you practise.
What makes this better than Metronome Beats or Pro Metronome?
Metro Gnome is the only free app that combines a precision metronome and a chromatic tuner that works in noisy rooms. Metronome Beats has no tuner at all. Pro Metronome switched paying customers to a subscription without warning. Metro Gnome has no subscription, no bait-and-switch, and a built-in rhythm game on top.
Is Metro Gnome good for music teachers?
Yes. The built-in practice timer lets you set a session goal (5, 10, 15, 20, or 30 minutes). When the session completes, the app tracks a daily streak and total sessions - so students build a practice habit and you can check in on their consistency. Recommend it to students as a free app they can use immediately, on any Android phone.
Does it work offline?
Fully. The metronome, tuner, and rhythm game all run entirely on-device - no internet connection required. The only feature that needs a connection is loading ads (which you can remove with a one-time purchase).
What Android version do I need?
Metro Gnome requires Android 7.0 (API level 24) or higher. It targets Android 13+ for the best experience but runs well on older devices too.
