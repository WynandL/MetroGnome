Contact Updated 22 November 2026

Contact Metro Gnome

The quickest way to reach us is email: accesscommsa@gmail.com. Every message is read by the person who builds the tools and the app.

What to write about

  • A tool gives a wrong or odd result. Tell us which tool, what you expected and what you saw. Your browser and device help too (for example "Chrome on a Windows laptop" or "Safari on an iPhone"), since audio timing differs between them.
  • A correction to an article. If a chord spelling, a time signature explanation or a figure looks wrong, say which page and which sentence. Corrections are made as soon as they are confirmed against a reference.
  • A tool you would like to see. Most of the tools on this site started as a question from a musician. Describe what you practise and what you would want the tool to tell you.
  • Teaching with the tools. Teachers are welcome to use the tools in lessons and to embed them on their own pages for free. Feedback on what works in a classroom is especially useful.
  • The Android app. For a problem in the app, include your phone model and the app version (shown in the app's Settings, under About).

What we do not need

Please never send passwords, payment details or other personal information. Purchases in the app are handled by Google Play, so refunds go through Google Play's own refund process.

Your privacy

Your email address is used only to reply to you and is never added to a mailing list or shared. The privacy policy explains what the site and the app collect.

More about who makes Metro Gnome, and how the tools are checked, is on the About page.
