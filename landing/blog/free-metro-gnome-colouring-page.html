Free Metro Gnome Colouring Page

Metro spends his days on a phone screen, so here is one of him you can print and colour in. Free, no sign-up, in A4 and US Letter.

Download A4 (PDF) Download US Letter (PDF)

Printing it

Both files are a single portrait page with a clean white margin, so any home printer will take them. Print at 100% (or "actual size") rather than "fit to page", which would shrink the margin for no reason.

Ordinary printer paper is fine for pencils and fine liners. If you would like to use markers or paint, a heavier sheet (120 gsm or more) stops the colour showing through the back.

A little about the picture

This is Metro in his workshop corner: pointed hat, spectacles, a tuning fork he has just struck against his boot, and a workbench with a globe, a quill and a stack of books. The curly moustache and the bow tie are the same ones he wears in the app.

Print as many copies as you like for yourself, a class or a studio waiting room. If you share it, please link to this page rather than re-hosting the file.

If you would like more, there is also a Metro Gnome inspired colouring book online with twenty musical woodland scenes. No need for it, though: this page is free and stays that way.
